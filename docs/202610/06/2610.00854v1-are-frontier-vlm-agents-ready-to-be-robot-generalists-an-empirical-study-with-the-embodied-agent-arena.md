## 摘要
前沿视觉语言模型（VLM）融合了场景估计、交互 grounding 与可执行动作。理解这些能力如何支持完整的机器人任务，是评估其作为机器人通才准备程度的核心。我们提出具身智能体竞技场（Embodied Agent Arena），以考察在几何、空间推理、可供性、任务规划与操作中，局部能力在何处支持了完整任务成功，又在何处未能支持。该竞技场包含 1,000 个案例，取自 32 个既有来源以及 GeoProbe——我们用于在 Blender 渲染图和真实场景图像上进行几何估计的新基准。一个最小化测试框架保留源观测与操作，同时分离度量精度、功能 grounding 与原生目标完成。我们评估了七个 VLM，分析 Astra 的任务特定优势，并比较更丰富观测的执行协议与多轮评审。在整个竞技场中，Astra 的优势在精确估计和可用接触定位上最为突出；完成协调的、目标导向的动作仍是实现机器人通用能力的关键差距。

## Abstract
Frontier vision-language models (VLMs) combine scene estimation, interaction grounding, and executable actions. Understanding how these abilities support complete robotic tasks is central to evaluating their readiness as robot generalists. We introduce Embodied Agent Arena to examine where local competence supports, or falls short of, complete task success across Geometry, Spatial Reasoning, Affordance, Task Planning, and Manipulation. The arena contains 1,000 cases drawn from 32 established sources and GeoProbe, our new benchmark for geometric estimation on Blender renders and real-scene images. A minimal harness preserves source observations and operations while separating metric precision, functional grounding, and native goal completion. We evaluate seven VLMs, analyze Astra's task-specific advantages, and compare richer-observation execution protocols and multi-round review. Across the arena, Astra's advantage is strongest in precise estimation and usable-contact localization; completing coordinated, goal-directed actions remains the key gap to robot generalism.
