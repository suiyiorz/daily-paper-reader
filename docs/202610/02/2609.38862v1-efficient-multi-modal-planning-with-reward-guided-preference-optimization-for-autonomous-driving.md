## 摘要
安全且高效的轨迹规划对自动驾驶至关重要。然而，现有的端到端方法往往在计算效率和安全保障方面均存在不足。基于模仿学习的方法受到因果混淆的影响，而基于规则的评分方法通常带来沉重的计算开销，并存在目标错位问题。此外，基于偏好的方法依赖严格的成对标注，限制了数据利用率。为克服这些局限，我们提出 EMPlan，一种由奖励引导微调驱动的高效多模态轨迹规划方法。我们设计了一种混合架构，将稀疏锚点与偏移精化模块相结合，以实现高效的多模态轨迹预测。稀疏锚点以低延迟提供粗略轨迹候选，随后由偏移模块进行精化，以获得更高的预测精度。为在不增加额外推理成本的情况下提升安全性，我们采用两阶段训练范式，包括预训练和奖励引导微调。在微调过程中，我们利用基于规则的奖励信号和非配对偏好监督，将预训练策略精炼至更安全的轨迹选择。我们在非反应式 NAVSIM 基准上评估 EMPlan，它在规划精度与效率之间取得了良好平衡，在实时约束下展现出优越性能。

## Abstract
Safe and efficient trajectory planning is essential in autonomous driving. However, existing end-to-end approaches often fall short in both computational efficiency and safety guarantees. Methods based on imitation learning suffer from causal confusion, while rule-based scoring approaches often incur heavy computational overhead and suffer from objective misalignment. Additionally, preference-based methods rely on strict pairwise annotations, limiting data utilization. To overcome these limitations, we propose EMPlan, an efficient multi-modal trajectory planning method powered by reward-guided fine-tuning. We design a hybrid architecture that combines sparse anchors with an offset refinement module for efficient multi-modal trajectory prediction. Sparse anchors provide coarse trajectory candidates with low latency, which are subsequently refined by the offset module for higher prediction accuracy. To enhance safety without incurring additional inference costs, we adopt a two-stage training paradigm consisting of pretraining and reward-guided fine-tuning. During fine-tuning, we leverage rule-based reward signals and unpaired preference supervision to refine the pretrained policy toward safer trajectory selection. We evaluate EMPlan on the non-reactive NAVSIM benchmark, where it strikes a favorable balance between planning accuracy and efficiency, demonstrating superior performance under real-time constraints.
