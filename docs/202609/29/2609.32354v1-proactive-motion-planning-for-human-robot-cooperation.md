## 摘要
本摘要探讨将人体运动预测融入主动式、动态的人类感知运动规划，目标是实现人与机器人之间的安全协作。采用一种基于图的深度学习模型来预测人体运动，并将其集成到一个规划框架中。该框架利用静态路线图以及时变A*算法来调整UR5e机械臂的轨迹。该方法通过将精确的运动预测与自适应轨迹规划相结合，显著改善了人机交互，并实现了主动避碰。

## Abstract
This abstract addresses the incorporation of human motion prediction into proactive and dynamic human-aware motion planning, with the goal of enabling safe collaboration between humans and robots. A deep learning, graph-based model is used to forecast human motion and is integrated into a planning framework. This framework employs a static roadmap along with a time-variant A* algorithm to modify the trajectory of a UR5e manipulator. This method greatly improves human-robot interaction and enables proactive collision avoidance by combining precise motion forecasts with adaptive trajectory planning.
