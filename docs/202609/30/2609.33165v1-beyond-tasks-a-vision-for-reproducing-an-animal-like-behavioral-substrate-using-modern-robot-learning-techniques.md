## 摘要
近期机器人学习的进展已催生出能力日益增强的具身智能体。然而，相对而言，人们较少关注动物持续展现出的一种更基本的能力：在物理、环境和社会需求随时间变化时，仍能保持身处情境之中、作出响应并维持行为连贯性的能力。我们提出将动物行为学意义上的行为基底作为一个概念透镜，用以研究人工智能体中的这类能力。我们认为，不应将动物表现出的这些行为视为一组孤立的技能；相反，它们在相互竞争的需求下持续协调，构成了现代机器人学习中一个重要但尚未充分探索的目标。我们进一步提出，机器人动物伴侣可作为研究以人为中心环境中持续交互与适应的有用研究场景。此类系统为研究社会行为、记忆和持续学习如何在长期交互中发展提供了机会。这一视角推动进一步研究这种持久的行为能力如何补充具身智能体中的更高层次能力。

## Abstract
Recent advances in robot learning have produced increasingly capable embodied agents. Yet comparatively less attention has been given to a more basic form of competence that animals exhibit continuously: the ability to remain situated, responsive, and behaviorally coherent as physical, environmental, and social demands change over time. We propose the ethological behavioral substrate as a conceptual lens for studying this form of competence in artificial agents. Rather than treating these behaviors that animals exhibit as a set of isolated skills, we argue that their continual coordination under competing demands constitutes an important and underexplored target for modern robot learning. We further propose robotic animal companions as a useful research setting for studying sustained interaction and adaptation in human-centered environments. Such systems provide an opportunity to investigate how social behavior, memory, and continual learning develop over long periods of interaction. This perspective motivates further investigation of how such persistent behavioral competence may complement higher-level capabilities in embodied agents.
